# **BCADM Community Code of Conduct**

## **Our Pledge**

We, the builders, experimenters, and maintainers of the **Bruce‑Constraint‑Aware‑Decision‑Module**, commit to shaping a community where every participant can engage without harassment, hostility, or exclusion.  
BCADM is a space for constraint‑aware thinking — and that applies to our social behavior as much as our code.

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds, identities, skill levels, and perspectives.  
Our goal is a community that is open, curious, respectful, and safe for everyone who steps into it.

## **Our Standards**

### **Positive Behaviors We Expect**

- Showing empathy, patience, and respect toward others  
- Engaging in discussions with curiosity rather than hostility  
- Offering constructive feedback and receiving it with maturity  
- Owning mistakes, correcting them, and learning from them  
- Prioritizing the health and growth of the community over personal ego

### **Unacceptable Behaviors**

- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention  
- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks  
- Trolling, baiting, or deliberately inflammatory comments  
- Publishing private information without explicit permission  
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional or collaborative environment

## **Enforcement Responsibilities**

Community moderators and maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.  
They may remove posts, comments, commits, or contributions that violate these standards.  
When moderation occurs, leaders will communicate the reasoning when appropriate.

## **Scope**

This Code of Conduct applies to all BCADM community spaces — repositories, discussions, issue trackers, wikis, and any official communication channels.  
It also applies when a member represents the BCADM project in public contexts, whether online or offline.

## **Reporting Issues**

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, you may report it to the BCADM moderation team at the designated contact email.  
Reports will be handled promptly, respectfully, and with confidentiality.

## **Enforcement Guidelines**

Moderators will use the following framework when determining consequences:

### **1. Correction**

**Impact:** Minor inappropriate behavior or language.  
**Consequence:** A private message explaining the issue and requesting improvement.

### **2. Warning**

**Impact:** A more significant violation or repeated minor issues.  
**Consequence:** A formal warning and temporary restriction from interacting with involved parties or specific community spaces.

### **3. Temporary Ban**

**Impact:** Serious misconduct or sustained inappropriate behavior.  
**Consequence:** Temporary removal from community participation. No interaction with affected individuals or moderators during this period.

### **4. Permanent Ban**

**Impact:** Repeated violations, harassment, or behavior that threatens the safety or integrity of the community.  
**Consequence:** Permanent removal from all BCADM community spaces.

## **Attribution**
